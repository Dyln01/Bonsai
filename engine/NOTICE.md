## Engine provenance

The `engine/` directory snapshots the modified `qwen36.c` (+ KV8 support files) from a
checkout of **colibri** (https://github.com/JustVugg/colibri) at commit `bf24429`,
Apache-2.0. It is provided for reproducibility of the measurements in `docs/`; upstream
owns the original engine. Backups: `qwen36.c.orig` (stock), `.premap` (pre-TRUNK_MAP),
`.prehot` (pre-hot-neuron) allow stepwise diffs.

## No-models notice

No real model weights, checkpoints, or tokenizer vocab files are included in this repo.
All benchmarks were produced with synthetic same-geometry containers generated by
`scripts/gen_*.py|sh`. The engine expects *converted* containers produced by colibri's own
converter from a model you obtain yourself under its own license.

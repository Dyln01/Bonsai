## Engine provenance

The `engine/` directory snapshots the modified `qwen36.c` (+ KV8 support files) from a
checkout of **colibri** (https://github.com/JustVugg/colibri) at commit `bf24429`,
Apache-2.0. It is provided for reproducibility of the measurements in `docs/`; upstream
owns the original engine. Backups: `qwen36.c.orig` (stock == bf24429 `c/qwen36.c`,
md5-verified), `.premap` (pre-TRUNK_MAP), `.prehot` (pre-hot-neuron), `.pretq`
(pre-KV_TQ), `.presm` (pre-FAST_SOFTMAX), `.premtp` (pre-MTP-part-1), `.predraft` (pre-MTP-part-2), `.preoracle` (pre-MTP-oracle), `.premoe` (pre-MTP-MoE-head) allow stepwise diffs.

`kv_fp8.h` is vendored **byte-identical** from colibri `bf24429` (`c/kv_fp8.h`,
Apache-2.0, (c) its authors) so the parity gates (`kv8_parity.c`, `kv8_fused.h`)
compile from this repo alone; CI does not need a colibri checkout for them.

Provenance is pinned in `tools/patch_manifest.json` and checked by
`tools/verify_engine.sh`: md5s of every snapshot plus a byte-exact replay of
the FULL patch chain (orig → premap → prehot → pretq → presm → qwen36.c),
also run in CI on every push. The E1 drift audit closed 2026-10-19 via two
regenerated patchers (`patch_kv8_flash_v1.py`, `patch_hotstack_v1.py`);
superseded historical per-feature scripts live in `tools/attic/`.

## No-models notice

No real model weights, checkpoints, or tokenizer vocab files are included in this repo.
All benchmarks were produced with synthetic same-geometry containers generated by
`scripts/gen_*.py|sh`. The engine expects *converted* containers produced by colibri's own
converter from a model you obtain yourself under its own license.
